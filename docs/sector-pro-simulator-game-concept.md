# Sector Pro Simulator – Game Concept Document

## Overview
Sector Pro Simulator is a single-player management and simulation experience that places players in charge of a live event production company. Inspired by the professional Sector Pro SaaS platform used by touring crews, the game translates complex scheduling, staffing, and logistics workflows into an engaging tycoon-style format. Players coordinate sound, lighting, and stage teams to deliver flawless gigs, tours, and festivals while balancing budgets, reputations, and unexpected crises.

## Genre & Audience
- **Genre:** Management simulation (tycoon) with strategic planning elements.
- **Target Platforms:** PC and Steam Deck, with an interface designed for mouse and keyboard but adaptable to controllers.
- **Primary Audience:** Players who enjoy deep simulation titles such as *Theme Hospital*, *Football Manager*, *Software Inc.*, and professionals curious about gamified production management. The tone remains professional yet approachable, welcoming newcomers without alienating industry veterans.

## Theme & Setting
- **Theme:** Backstage technical production of live entertainment.
- **Setting:** Contemporary venues ranging from intimate clubs to massive outdoor festivals. The aesthetic is grounded and realistic with a lighthearted edge to make complex systems approachable.
- **Player Fantasy:** Grow a scrappy local crew into an internationally renowned production powerhouse capable of staging multi-stage festivals under extreme pressure.

## Core Value Proposition
1. **Authenticity with Accessibility** – Real-world terminology, roles, and logistical challenges based on Sector Pro workflows are simplified into digestible mechanics. Players learn about power loads, technical riders, and crew coordination without needing industry expertise.
2. **Strategy Meets Creativity** – Budget spreadsheets and roster planning coexist with expressive elements such as stage layout design and festival site planning, appealing to both analytical and creative playstyles.
3. **Progressive Challenge Curve** – Early gigs focus on single-night shows; later contracts introduce multi-city tours and sprawling festivals with layered crises, ensuring long-term engagement.
4. **Behind-the-Scenes Niche** – Whereas other tycoon games focus on performers or venues, Sector Pro Simulator highlights the unsung heroes—roadies, riggers, and engineers—filling a fresh niche in the market.

## Pillars of Play
- **Plan Like a Pro:** Master calendars, crew availability, and resource allocation.
- **Execute Under Pressure:** Navigate real-time crises with pausable controls.
- **Grow the Business:** Invest in staff, equipment, and technology to unlock larger opportunities.
- **Maintain Reputation:** Satisfied clients and smooth shows fuel exponential growth.

## Competitive Landscape
Sector Pro Simulator differentiates itself by adapting a real industry tool into an entertaining format. Comparable titles include *Festival Tycoon* (focus on festival management) and *Not For Broadcast* (broadcast control room chaos), but no existing product centers on live technical production management. This creates a clear positioning opportunity.

## Project Scope Snapshot
- **MVP Vision:** A polished vertical slice featuring single-venue gigs, light crew management, and a complete plan → execute → evaluate loop.
- **Full Release Goal:** Multiple event types, deep crew systems, and robust financial modeling to satisfy simulation aficionados.
- **Long-Term Potential:** Modular expansions (weather, rivals, co-op) to keep the simulation evolving post-launch.
