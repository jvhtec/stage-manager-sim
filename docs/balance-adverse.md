# Adverse scenarios

Generated by `npm run balance:adverse` — GB 1995 and ES 1990, seeds 3, 4, 6 years each. In year 2 of an established company, one thing goes badly wrong — or all four at once — and the run is compared with the same seed left alone.

- **Biggest client walks**: the act that brought in the most money over the past year signs with a rival. Its booked shows are cancelled on their contract terms (deposits and cancellation clauses pay out, if you have them) and it won’t book you for two years.
- **Fleet recall**: the maker calls in the model most of the fleet runs on. Those trucks finish the trip they’re on and sit at the dealer for 12 days; shows booked onto them need another truck.
- **Economic downturn**: 18 months of 30% less work at fees 15% lower; defaults double and clients cancel more while demand is down.
- **Late-paying promoters**: for a year, invoices go late three times as often, and up to twice as late.
