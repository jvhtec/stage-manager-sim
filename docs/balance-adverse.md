# Adverse scenarios

Generated by `npm run balance:adverse` — GB 1995 and ES 1990, seeds 3, 4, 5, 8 years each. 4.3 years in, once the company is established, one thing goes badly wrong — or all four within two months — and the run is compared with the same seed left alone. Up to the shock the two are the same game move for move, so the twelve months after it show the damage itself; the end-of-run column also carries every other way two eight-year runs drift apart.

- **Biggest client walks**: the act that paid the most this year and last (counting what it has booked) signs with a rival. Its booked shows are cancelled on their contract terms (deposits and cancellation clauses pay out, if you have them) and it won’t book you for two years.
- **Fleet recall**: the maker calls in the model most of the fleet runs on. Those trucks finish the trip they’re on and sit at the dealer for 12 days; shows booked onto them need another truck.
- **Economic downturn**: 18 months of 30% less work at fees 15% lower; defaults double and clients cancel more while demand is down.
- **Late-paying promoters**: for a year, invoices go late three times as often, and up to twice as late.
