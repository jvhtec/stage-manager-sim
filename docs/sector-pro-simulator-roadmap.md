# Sector Pro Simulator – Feature Roadmap

## Development Philosophy
As a solo-developed project, Sector Pro Simulator prioritizes incremental releases that steadily expand depth without overwhelming production capacity. Each milestone builds upon the previous one, ensuring that players receive a coherent, polished experience at every stage.

## Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
A focused vertical slice demonstrating the core contract → planning → execution loop.

### Gameplay Scope
- Single-day gig contracts with clear success criteria.
- Simplified crew hiring and assignment (limited roster, no advanced fatigue system).
- Basic equipment selection with owned vs. rented differentiation kept minimal.
- Linear calendar supporting one active contract at a time.
- Lightweight crisis events (e.g., minor equipment failure) to test responsiveness.

### Systems & UI
- Event planning screen with checklists and drag-and-drop assignments.
- Crew roster view with essential stats and availability indicators.
- Financial tracker showing income, expenses, and bankruptcy fail-state.
- Prototype isometric event view using placeholder art to visualize execution.
- Core HUD elements: time controls, alert feed, and progress meters.

### Deliverables
- Playable demo allowing multiple consecutive gigs.
- Basic tutorial prompts introducing UI and terminology.
- Telemetry hooks or feedback forms for early player insights.

## Version 1.0 Launch
Expands the simulation into a robust tycoon experience covering the full spectrum of live event production.

### Major Additions
- **Event Variety:** Introduce multi-city tours and multi-stage festivals with dedicated planning tools.
- **Deep Crew Simulation:** Skill progression, fatigue/morale systems, certifications, and interpersonal events.
- **Equipment Management:** Ownership lifecycle, maintenance, wear, and specialized gear affecting outcomes.
- **Economy Enhancements:** Loans, sponsorships, variable seasonal pricing, and multiple win conditions.
- **Advanced Tools:** AI rider analysis, power load calculators, and scheduling automation upgrades.
- **Content Expansion:** Additional venue archetypes, regional travel map, unique client personalities.
- **Polish & Accessibility:** Full art pass, audio design, localization support, tutorials, and QoL features like search, filters, and automation toggles.

### Quality Targets
- Stable performance on mid-range PCs.
- Comprehensive balancing of finances and crisis frequency.
- Clear roadmap communication for post-launch content to retain community interest.

## Post-Launch Expansion Ideas
Prioritized based on community feedback and resource availability.

### Systems Enhancements
- Dynamic weather simulation affecting outdoor shows (rain, wind, heatwaves).
- Rival production companies introducing competitive bidding and AI-driven calendar conflicts.
- Cooperative or competitive multiplayer modes sharing contract pools.

### Content Packs
- Narrative campaign arcs with voiced characters and branching decisions.
- Venue ownership/management expansion, letting players renovate and profit from their own spaces.
- Artist management crossover where players coordinate performers alongside technical crews.

### Community & Modding
- Modding tools for custom events, gear, and crisis scenarios.
- Steam Workshop integration for sharing scenarios and UI skins.
- Live events, seasonal challenges, or limited-time scenarios to encourage return play.

## Milestone Planning
| Milestone | Duration | Focus | Key Deliverables |
|-----------|----------|-------|------------------|
| Pre-Production | 2–3 months | Prototyping & design | MVP spec, art direction guide, technology decisions |
| Vertical Slice | 3–4 months | Core loop implementation | Playable demo, feedback collection, marketing teaser |
| Alpha | 4 months | Feature completion | Tours/festivals, expanded systems, first-pass balance |
| Beta | 3 months | Polish & optimization | Final art/audio, full testing, localization strings |
| Launch Prep | 1 month | Release readiness | Marketing assets, store page updates, day-one patch plan |

## Risk Management
- **Scope Creep:** Maintain strict feature gatekeeping per milestone; defer extras to expansions.
- **Content Bottlenecks:** Use modular templates for events, crises, and clients to streamline authoring.
- **Technical Overload:** Establish reusable UI components and automate testing for scheduling conflicts.
- **Player Onboarding:** Continue playtesting tutorials and glossary to ensure industry terms remain understandable.

## Success Metrics
- Positive retention in post-launch telemetry (players completing multiple in-game months).
- Favorable reviews citing authenticity, depth, and usability.
- Sustainable cadence of updates post-release without compromising solo-developer bandwidth.
